      Spaceflight hazards

      Source: NASA HRP · 5 Hazards of Human Spaceflight

      NASA's Human Research Program groups spaceflight risks into five hazards. Each card lists the daily indicators the hazard may affect, with their latest status. These are possible influences, not certain cause and effect.

      Alertness

      Reaction self-test

      Inspired by the psychomotor vigilance test (PVT) used on the ISS. For 1 minute, tap the circle or press Space as soon as it lights up. The wait between lights is random, so don't anticipate.

      Not a validated clinical test. Run it at the same time each day, in a quiet spot. In microgravity, brace yourself with a foot restraint and hold the device steady.
